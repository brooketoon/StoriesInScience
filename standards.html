How alignment is handled

Three dimensions, not just a code.

A lesson isn’t aligned because it shares a topic with a standard. It’s aligned because students do the thinking the standard describes.

Content

The disciplinary core idea sits inside a problem that requires it, rather than appearing as vocabulary to memorise ahead of an activity.

Practice

Each lesson names the Science and Engineering Practice it exercises and builds a task that genuinely requires it — modelling, analysing data, arguing from evidence.

Crosscutting concept

Patterns, cause and effect, systems, and stability and change are the lenses students are explicitly asked to apply when interpreting what they found.
